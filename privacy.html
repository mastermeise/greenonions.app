Green Onions

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 July 2026

Green Onions collects nothing, sends nothing, and stores nothing about you.

Green Onions is an iPhone app that plays music already stored on your device and adjusts its tempo to match your running cadence. It is designed to work entirely offline, and it does exactly that.

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. Specifically, Green Onions has:

  • No user accounts and no sign-up
  • No analytics, telemetry, or crash-reporting services
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data
  • No server of our own — there is nowhere for your data to be sent

Your music library

To do its job, Green Onions asks permission to access the music on your device. It reads your songs so it can analyze their tempo and play them back. This analysis happens entirely on your iPhone. Your library, your song list, and your listening habits are never uploaded anywhere and are never visible to us.

If you decline the permission, the app simply cannot play from your library. You can change your mind at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Media & Apple Music.

Data stored on your device

Green Onions saves your settings and its tempo analysis of your songs locally on your iPhone, so it does not have to re-analyze the same track twice. This information stays in the app's own storage. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Children

Green Onions does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects this policy, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.

Contact
