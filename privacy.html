Green Onions

Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 31, 2026

The short version

Green Onions does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data.

There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party services in the app. Everything the app stores stays on your device. That's the whole policy — the rest is just detail. (This website is a separate thing from the app; it's covered at the bottom.)

What the app accesses on your device

To do its job — playing your music at a tempo matched to your cadence — Green Onions needs access to music you choose to bring in:

  • Music you import from Files. When you add songs from the Files app, Green Onions copies those audio files into its own storage on your device.
  • Your local media library and iTunes purchases (only with your permission). If you choose to import from your device's music library, iOS asks for your permission first. When granted, Green Onions copies the songs you specifically pick into its own storage so it can analyze and tempo-match them offline. It does not read, upload, or catalog your wider library. Apple Music subscription downloads are DRM-protected and cannot be imported at all.

In both cases the music stays on your device. It is never uploaded anywhere.

What the app collects

Nothing. Green Onions has no servers and makes no network connections. It does not collect personal information, usage data, location, contacts, identifiers, or diagnostics. Your song library, your detected and edited BPM values, and your pace settings are stored only in the app's local storage on your device.

Accounts

There are none. Green Onions does not require or offer account creation, and there is no login.

Third parties

The app includes no third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or software development kits. No data is shared with anyone, because no data is collected. This website is a different matter, and it's described below.

Children

Green Onions does not collect personal data from users of any age. No personal information is ever gathered, from anyone.

Data storage and deletion

All app data lives locally on your device. You are in full control:

  • Delete individual songs from within the app's Library at any time.
  • Deleting the Green Onions app from your device removes all of its data permanently.

Apple

Green Onions is distributed through Apple's App Store. Any diagnostics or usage information you have chosen to share with Apple at the system level are handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy, not by Green Onions. The app itself receives none of it.

This website

greenonions.app is separate from the app, and unlike the app it does rely on two outside services. Neither one has any connection to the app, and neither can see anything about your music.

  • Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page visits, so I can tell whether anyone is finding this site. It sets no cookies, stores no identifiers, and doesn't follow you to any other website. It can't identify you.
  • Google Fonts serves the typeface these pages are set in. Your browser requests that font file from Google, which sees your IP address — the same as it would for any font or image hosted elsewhere.

There is no advertising here, no tracking pixels, and nothing that follows you off this site. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps its own standard server logs.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date, and any material change will ship with a clear notice in the app. What the app does with your data will always be described here accurately and in plain language.

Contact
