The idea

Match the beat to your feet

Every running app makes you run to their playlist. This one runs to yours.

Tempo-matching apps hand you a catalog and a subscription. Playlist apps just sort songs into BPM buckets and hope something fits. Green Onions does the harder thing: it analyzes the tracks already on your phone and time-stretches them to your exact cadence as you run — so a song at 148 BPM becomes a song at 165 BPM, still in key, still sounding like itself.

The surprise

Your whole library is running music

That was the part I didn't expect. When any song can meet your cadence, "running music" stops being a genre you tolerate and starts being the music you actually love. Records that were never remotely workout material turn out to be perfect — they just needed to arrive at the right tempo.

Mid-run

Turn it up a notch

You're in the home stretch and you want to push. Reach down, tap + right on the lock screen, and the music comes with you — the tempo climbs to the pace you're about to run. No unlocking, no fumbling, no stopping.

Who it's for

Runners
